Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your account details (email and password) and the financial transaction data you enter to track your expenses. We may also collect basic technical data such as your browser type and IP address for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes managing your account, calculating your spending totals, and displaying your financial history. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your data with third-party services unless required by law or to provide a core feature of the application that you have explicitly consented to.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channels provided in your account profile.
